## Clothespin Box with Clothesline Clips

A 3D-printable clothespin holder that clips directly onto your clothesline
instead of sitting loose on top of the drying rack or getting knocked off
by the wind. No more chasing a fallen bag of pegs across the balcony.

### The problem

Most clothespin bags either hang from a single hook (and slide around) or
just sit balanced somewhere on the rack, ready to tip over the moment you
bump it. I wanted something that clips onto the line itself, stays put
while I'm hanging laundry, and is easy to grab a peg from one-handed.

### The design

The box is an open, tapered basket with rounded corners — wide enough to
scoop a handful of clothespins out without fishing around, and shaped so
it doesn't dig into your hand when you're carrying it. On the back, two
clip parts grab the clothesline (or a drying rack bar) so the box hangs
securely at whatever height is convenient, and moves with you as you work
down the line.

It's split into two separate parts so each prints cleanly with no
supports:

- **Box** — the basket body
- **Clamp** — the line clip (print 2x)
